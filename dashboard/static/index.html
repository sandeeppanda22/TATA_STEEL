Vendor Target

Target = what must be done by the To date to be on schedule, measured against actual progress: backlog (planned-to-From minus actual) + Next Planned (further work scheduled up to To that isn't already done). Activities that are ahead of schedule need less or none. Default window: today to 30 days ahead.

Bottlenecks

An activity is flagged if it is behind planned-to-date by more than 15 percentage, is holding up the next activity in sequence, or has had zero recorded progress for 3+ weeks since its start date.

Milestones

Completed 25/50/100% marks per activity, plus a 30-day look-ahead (planned end date, and a progress-rate projection) for activities not yet finished.
